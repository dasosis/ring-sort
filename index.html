Ring Sort Solver

Sort every colour onto its own rod. One spare rod, top ring moves only, any rod with space.

How it works

  1. Consolidate. Empty the emptiest rod into the gaps elsewhere, so one rod E is empty and the rest are full.
  2. Pick a colour c that is cheapest to extract.
  3. Partition. For each full rod A holding c, use E and a borrowed full rod B to rebuild A with its c rings on top. B is restored exactly and E ends empty (at most 4h moves).
  4. Pour. Lift every c ring off the tops into E. There are exactly h of them, so E is finished. Repeat with one colour fewer.

Each phase with k colours left costs at most (4k + 2)·h moves, so the total is O(n²·h) moves and O(n²·h) time, linear in the length of the answer. It always succeeds but isn't minimum-move: finding the fewest moves for this family of puzzles is NP-hard.
